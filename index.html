About the Space

Everything Your Family Needs

Tucked between the Gulf of Mexico and the Intracoastal Waterway in Indian Rocks/Shores, this freshly renovated 3-story townhouse delivers the full Florida experience. New floors, updated kitchens and baths, coastal décor — and a host who's thought of everything, right down to the boogie boards and fishing poles.

  • Fully stocked kitchen with cookware, condiments & coffee
  • Beach essentials: chairs, umbrella, cart & boogie boards
  • Smart TVs + high-speed WiFi throughout
  • In-unit washer & dryer
  • Dock with fish cleaning station & kayak launch
  • Fishing poles provided for the whole crew
  • Half bath on main level for convenience
  • ~10 miles to Clearwater/St. Pete Airport (PIE)
